THE TI WEST INCIDENT

When director Ti West submitted his 2022 Sight & Sound ballot, he included Jaws and Citizen Kane. The internet tore him apart. Why? They were deemed "too basic". He wasn't performing enough cinephilic obscurity. The signal mattered more than the films themselves.
