WHAT LETTERBOXD SELLS YOU

Community. Discovery. Democratisation.

Before, film criticism belonged to the gatekeepers. The broadsheet critics, the academics, the festival elites. Then came a platform that flattened the hierarchy. Everyone is a critic now. The irreverent one-liner shares the same screen space as the 1,500-word academic essay. The gatekeepers lost their keys, and the democratisation of taste was complete.

THE MAP EATS THE TERRITORY.

Philosopher C. Thi Nguyen describes a phenomenon called "value capture." It happens when we take a rich, complex, unquantifiable value — like the love of cinema — and replace it with a simplified metric. Over time, we stop optimising for the experience itself. We start optimising for the metric.

Taste is never just private. As sociologist Pierre Bourdieu argued, taste is a weapon used to classify ourselves and others. The 'Top Four' is the ultimate modern expression of cultural capital. It's a carefully curated billboard of who we want people to think we are.

"Director Ti West submitted his Sight & Sound ballot. He put Jaws and Citizen Kane. The internet tore him apart. Why? Because they were deemed 'too basic'. He wasn't performing enough cinephilic obscurity."

"The value being performed here is not aesthetic discernment. It is the performance of aesthetic discernment."
