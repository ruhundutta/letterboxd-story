THE QUANTIFICATION OF LOVE

Philosopher C. Thi Nguyen describes a phenomenon called value capture. It happens when we take a rich, complex, unquantifiable human value—like a visceral connection to cinema—and replace it with a simplified metric ecosystem. Over time, we stop optimizing for the transformative art experience itself. We start tracking, logging, and performance-tuning the score metric matrix.
